Welcome to Psycho Astronaut (SAI-ko AS-tro-nót). ...

Episode zero.
The manifesto. ...

This channel is experimental engineering
with a budget of zero dollars. ...
We call it conscious jank (KÓN-chus DJÉNK).
Duct tape,
but with version control. ...

Rule one.
Everything runs locally.
No cloud.
No subscriptions.
One graphics card. ...

This video was not edited by a person. ...

It was assembled by a Python (PAI-thon) pipeline. ...

Step one.
Faster Whisper (FÁS-ter UÍS-per) listens to my voice
and writes down every word.
Step two.
An index reads every asset I own,
and labels it.
Step three.
SigLIP two (SIG-lip tu) matches each sentence to a picture,
by meaning. ...
Then a local Qwen (TCHU-en) model, running on Ollama (o-LÁ-ma),
plans the cuts,
five beats at a time.
Hungarian (RAN-gá-ri-an) assignment makes sure the same photo never shows up twice.
Mostly. ...

Stock footage?
Only public domain, with a license audit.
Lawyers, relax.
Backgrounds are removed by Rembg BiRefNet (REM-bi-gí BAI-ref-nét).
A quality gate rejects the ugly ones.
Real ESRGAN (RÍ-al ES-AR-gan) upscales the blurry photos.
Vidstab (VÍD-stab) calms the shaky clips. ...

Then FFmpeg (éf-éf-ÉM-peg) renders everything on NVENC (en-VÉNK),
with cache and transitions.
Loudnorm (LAUD-norm) fixes the volume.
The terminal theme adds scanlines and glitch. ...

Proof?
This is a video about the Hubble servicing mission.
Same pipeline.
Real NASA footage,
all public domain. ...
It is not perfect.
Sometimes I say telescope,
and it shows a decorative mirror
with flowers. ...

And yes,
it crashes sometimes. ...
Out of memory.
Of course.
That is the whole show. ...

But it is not fully automated.
Not even close. ...
The ideas are human.
The script is human.
The voice is human,
sorry. ...
The pipeline only does the boring part.
I decide what to say.
The machine decides where the pictures go. ...

All of it,
on a single RTX (AR-ti-éks) three thousand sixty. ...
The code is open.
The footage is public domain.
The budget is zero. ...
Subscribe,
or don't.
The pipeline will not take it personally.
